Privacy Policy

UpMind  ·  Last updated: September 2026
☀️

This Privacy Policy applies to UpMind, a personal productivity and wellness tracker (tasks, habits, sport, goals, and finance). We respect your privacy. UpMind does not require registration or a user account, and we do not build advertising profiles about you. Most of your data stays on your device; a few optional features send data to trusted service providers, as described below.

1. Information We Collect

UpMind does not require user registration and does not collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes. To provide its features, the app uses the following device capabilities:

  • Microphone — to capture your speech when you add an entry by voice. Speech is converted to text by your device's operating system (Apple or Google) speech recognition; the resulting text is then processed as described in Section 2. UpMind does not store your audio.
  • Camera & Photos — to let you attach images to tasks, goals, and your vision board. Attachments are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Notifications — to deliver reminders (task deadlines, regular payments, and motivational messages). Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; notification settings are stored locally.

2. AI Features & Data Processing

UpMind includes optional AI features:

  • Quick Add (voice or text) — you describe an entry in your own words and the app structures it into a task, expense, income, habit, goal, debt, payment, or sport activity.
  • Focus insights — the app reviews how complete your sections are for the week and suggests where to focus.
  • Goal breakdown — the app can suggest sub-steps for a goal you create.

When you use these features, the text you type or dictate — and, for Focus, your per-section completion percentages and app language — are sent over a secure connection to Google's Gemini models via Firebase AI Logic for processing, and the structured result is returned to you. Requests are protected by Firebase App Check.

This input may include personal information that you choose to enter (for example, task descriptions, amounts, or notes). Please avoid entering information you do not wish to be processed. We use this content only to provide the AI features; we do not use it for advertising or profiling. Google processes the data as our service provider in accordance with its own terms. Because these features use the Gemini Developer API, submitted content may be used by Google to provide and improve its services; do not submit sensitive information you would not want processed for that purpose. The AI features are optional — you can use the rest of UpMind without them.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information involved only to:

  • Structure your voice or text input into entries and provide Focus insights and goal suggestions
  • Deliver reminders you have enabled
  • Respond to your support requests and improve the app

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data with trusted service providers only as necessary to operate the app and provide its features.

4. Data Storage

Your app data — tasks, habits, anti-habits, sport, goals, finance, accounts, savings, debts, regular payments, vision board, and settings — is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. You can delete this data at any time from within the app or by uninstalling UpMind. If you export a backup, the exported file contains your data and is shared only to the destination you choose.

5. Firebase Services

UpMind uses Google Firebase services: Firebase AI Logic (to provide the AI features described in Section 2), Firebase App Check (to protect those requests from abuse), and Cloud Firestore (to store feedback you choose to send). UpMind does not use Firebase for user accounts, and reminders are handled by on-device scheduling rather than cloud push messaging.

6. Feedback

When you choose to send feedback from within the app, we collect the message you write, the feedback category you select, and basic technical details (your device platform and language). This information is stored in Cloud Firestore and forwarded to our support email through a third-party automation service (Make.com) so we can read and respond. We use it only to understand issues and improve UpMind; we do not use it for advertising.

7. Exchange Rates

If you choose a currency other than the default, the app fetches current exchange rates from a third-party rates service over a secure connection. This request contains no personal data — only the currency information needed to display converted amounts.

8. Subscriptions & Donations

UpMind offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly) via the App Store or Google Play. Purchases and subscription status are processed by the respective store and by our subscription provider (RevenueCat); we do not receive your payment card details.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings at any time. Donations are voluntary and do not unlock any features.

9. Third-Party Services

UpMind relies on trusted third-party service providers to support features such as AI processing (Google), abuse protection (Firebase App Check), feedback handling (Cloud Firestore and Make.com), exchange rates, and subscriptions and payment services provided by the respective app store. These providers process data only as necessary to provide the requested functionality and in accordance with their own privacy practices.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Children's Privacy

UpMind is not directed at children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored data by clearing it in the app or uninstalling UpMind. You may also request assistance, including deletion of any feedback you have sent, by contacting us at ipowerstore44@gmail.com.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
